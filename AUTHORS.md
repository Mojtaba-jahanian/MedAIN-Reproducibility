## Reproducibility Scope

This repository provides research materials associated with MedAIN.

The manuscript reports that acquisition-sensitivity attenuation does not necessarily improve external segmentation performance. The reported results should not be interpreted as establishing general superiority over strong domain-generalization baselines.

Historical Stage 9B results have provenance limitations, including unavailable original run-level artifacts. The repository must not be described as a complete end-to-end reproduction package unless all required files have been independently verified.

Original clinical images are governed by their respective dataset providers' access and reuse conditions.
